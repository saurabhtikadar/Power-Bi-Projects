# Ola Project Analysis using SQL and Power BI

This project demonstrates the analysis of Ola ride data using SQL for data processing and Power BI for interactive visualization. The analysis provides insights into ride volume, booking trends, customer behavior, and revenue generation, offering a comprehensive understanding of the operational and business aspects of Ola.

---

## 🚀 **Project Highlights**

- **Technologies Used:**  
  - **SQL :** Data extraction, transformation, and analysis.  
  - **Power BI :** Interactive dashboards and visual storytelling.
  - **PowerPoint :** For designing polished and professional dashboard looks for presentations.

- **Key Business Questions Addressed:**  
  1. Ride Volume Over Time  
  2. Booking Status Breakdown  
  3. Vehicle Types by Ride Distance  
  4. Average Customer Ratings by Vehicle Type  
  5. Cancelled Rides Reasons (Customer vs. Driver)  
  6. Revenue by Payment Method  
  7. Top 5 Customers by Total Booking Value  
  8. Ride Distance Distribution Per Day  
  9. Driver Ratings Distribution  
  10. Customer vs. Driver Ratings  

---

## 📊 **Dashboard Segregation**

The Power BI dashboard is divided into five key views for ease of navigation and focused analysis:

### **1. Overall Analysis**
   - **Ride Volume Over Time:** Trend analysis of rides completed during the observation period.  
   - **Booking Status Breakdown:** Distribution of Success, cancelled by driver,cancelled by customer and Driver not found.  

### **2. Vehicle Type Insights**
   - **Vehicle Types by Ride Distance:** Identify the vehicle types based on ride distances.  

### **3. Revenue Insights**
   - **Revenue by Payment Method:** Analyze revenue distribution across various payment methods.  
   - **Top 5 Customers by Total Booking Value:** Spotlight on high-value customers.  
   - **Ride Distance Distribution Per Day:** Understand the spread of ride distances on a daily basis.  

### **4. Cancellation Analysis**
   - **Cancelled Rides Reasons (Customer):** Insights into why customers cancel rides.  
   - **Cancelled Rides Reasons (Driver):** Reasons drivers cancel rides.  

### **5. Ratings Analysis**
   - **Driver Ratings Distribution:** Distribution of ratings given to drivers.  
   - **Customer Ratings Distribution:** Feedback scores given by drivers to customers.  
   - **Customer vs. Driver Ratings:** Comparative analysis of rating patterns.

1. **Data Preparation:**  
   - Data extraction and transformation using SQL queries.
